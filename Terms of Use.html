3. Your location and safety decisions

Check the selected place and the time of the last update. A saved GPS point may be old after you move. A searched town represents an approximate town centre, not a street address or your live position. Broad forecasts, flood proxies, earthquake reports and regional warnings do not establish the risk at a particular property. HorizonCue does not predict earthquakes.

Use official warnings and your own assessment of conditions when deciding whether to travel, evacuate, shelter or seek help. Do not use the app as your only source for a safety-critical decision. Preparation checklists are general suggestions; local authority instructions and your personal circumstances take priority.

4. Emergency numbers and SOS drafts

Displayed emergency numbers are based on the selected app place, which may differ from your actual location. Check the country you are physically in. The call button opens your phone's dialer; you must place the call. The SOS feature prepares an SMS or email draft for a contact you choose; it does not send automatically, confirm delivery or contact emergency services for you. Check the recipient, location and message before sending. For an immediate emergency, call emergency services directly.
