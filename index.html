2. Data used by the app

Places and GPS

You can search for a town or ask the app to use GPS. A town search sends the text you type to Open-Meteo. If you choose GPS and grant Android's location permission, the app obtains a fix at that moment. It stores the selected point, a place label, and, for GPS, the reported accuracy and fix time on your phone. Coordinates used in online location and weather requests are rounded to four decimal places. Rounding reduces detail slightly but can still reveal a small area; it is not anonymisation.

The app does not continuously follow your movements and does not request background-location permission. A saved GPS point stays at its last fix until you tap GPS again. Saved places may still be checked for forecasts in the background, even after you close the app. Revoking Android's location permission stops new GPS fixes; remove a saved GPS place as well if you want its forecast requests to stop.

Information kept on your phone

HorizonCue uses Android app storage for saved places and labels, forecast and hazard cards, events you flag, past-event summaries, preparation tasks and ticks, notification choices and history, recent GPS accuracy and time, and temporary place-name results. Its home-screen widget reads that local data. Saved event snapshots remain until you remove them, even if a live event disappears or you remove its place.

The app does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts or email address to provide weather alerts. It has no app account, ad SDK or analytics SDK. Android app backup is disabled in the current demo.

Requests go directly from your device to these services over HTTPS. A provider receives your public IP address and technical request details as part of the connection. Where a request contains a point, it may also receive the rounded coordinates. We do not receive a copy of the provider's request logs and cannot erase them from its systems.

Provider retention and locations

Open-Meteo says it removes individual free-API webserver logs, which can include coordinates, after 90 days. The OpenStreetMap Foundation describes itself as an independent controller and publishes details of its request logging; its Nominatim systems are in the UK, Ireland and the Netherlands, while map tiles use a global delivery network. BigDataCloud says its data is stored in Australia and the US and describes deletion or de-identification when data is no longer needed; it does not give a fixed period for this request type. RainViewer and the public hazard providers have their own published practices and do not provide us with a single uniform retention period. Follow the provider links above for their current terms or contact them about their logs.

International processing: Some requests may be handled outside the European Economic Area. Switzerland and the UK have European Commission adequacy decisions; that does not establish a transfer basis for every provider or country listed here. The public demo does not have an app-specific data-processing or transfer agreement with these public API operators. We do not claim an EU-only route or a verified transfer safeguard for every service.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Places, events, tasks and settings: stored on your phone until you remove them, use Settings → Delete saved app data, or uninstall. A flagged event must be removed separately if you want to keep other app data.
  • Notification history: stored on your phone until cleared, deleted with all saved app data, or uninstalled; the app keeps at most the latest 80 records.
  • GPS place-name cache: reused for up to 24 hours. Old entries may remain in local app storage until all saved app data is deleted or the app is uninstalled.
  • Emergency-number lookup cache: a country result is reused for up to seven days. Old entries may remain locally until all saved app data is deleted or the app is uninstalled.
  • Radar map-tile cache: base map tiles are reused for up to seven days; expired files may remain in Android's app cache until replaced, Android clears the cache, you clear app storage in Android settings, or you uninstall. The in-app Delete saved app data button does not clear these tile files.
  • Ordinary support and feedback email: if you send us a message, we will delete it within 12 months of the most recent message in the conversation, unless a longer period is required for a legal claim or obligation.
  • Privacy-rights correspondence: kept for up to three years after a request is closed to document our response, then deleted unless a longer legal need applies.

Deleting information on your phone does not recall an SOS, feedback or issue-report message you chose to send, and does not delete logs held by external services. We do not keep a separate HorizonCue account or location database on our own server.

6. SOS, email and sharing

The SOS feature opens an SMS or email draft containing the selected coordinates, a map link and place details. For a GPS place it also describes the age and reported accuracy of the saved fix; for a searched town it says it is a town centre, not your live position. You choose the recipient and decide whether to send. HorizonCue does not read your contacts or send the message itself. The messaging app, its provider and the recipient handle a sent message under their own terms.

Send feedback and Report an issue open an email draft to info.cork.business@gmail.com. The app does not automatically attach diagnostics or location. If you send it, we receive your address, message and any details you add in a Google-hosted Gmail inbox; Google's privacy policy applies to its service. Please do not use this address for an emergency.

The app's share button uses Android's share sheet. You choose the destination app and whether to send. HorizonCue does not independently send a share message.

7. Your privacy choices and rights

You can search for a town instead of using GPS, remove saved places and flagged events, disable notification categories, clear history, revoke location or notification permission in Android settings, delete saved app data inside HorizonCue, or uninstall. To clear map-tile files as well, use Android's Settings → Apps → HorizonCue → Storage → Clear storage or uninstall; wording varies by device.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw optional GPS permission at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Email info.cork.business@gmail.com and tell us what you need. Because the main app records stay on your phone, we may not be able to identify or retrieve them centrally; use the in-app controls for those records. We will respond to rights requests within the applicable legal timeframe.

You may complain to a data-protection authority. In Ireland this is the Data Protection Commission.
